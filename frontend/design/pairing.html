What pairing does

and, just as importantly, what it does not
  • [+] node-04 receives a unique, revocable credential Issued to this pairing only. Revoking it invalidates that credential and nothing else.
  • [+] It advertises verified hardware, runtime, model, health and capability data The coordinator routes bounded jobs on that evidence while trust remains active.
  • [+] The coordinator may send it bounded job context Only what a step needs. It writes inside an assigned temporary workspace and nowhere else.
  • [−] It does not merge or replace either workspace Chats, memory, files, rules and artifacts stay where they are, on both sides.
  • [−] It does not become the owner of this workspace Canonical writes stay with the coordinator that owns the workspace.
  • [−] It does not gain a permanent role Coordinator, worker and server are runtime responsibilities. Disconnecting returns both installs to ordinary local operation with no data migration.
