SOP-MECH-014 rev 3
ROUTINE MECHANICAL INSPECTION OF TRANSFER PUMPS

1. SCOPE

This procedure covers routine mechanical inspection of transfer pumps in the
P-200 series. It does not cover overhaul, alignment or electrical testing.

2. ACCEPTANCE LIMITS

2.1  Vibration velocity at any bearing housing shall not exceed 7.1 mm/s RMS.
     A reading above this limit is an alarm condition.

2.2  Bearing temperature shall not exceed 80 C measured at the housing.

2.3  Where a reading cannot be taken, the field shall be recorded as
     "not recorded" together with the reason. A missing reading shall never be
     entered as zero, as in specification, or inferred from a previous report.

--- PAGE 2 ---

3. RESPONSE TO AN ALARM CONDITION

3.1  A vibration alarm requires re-inspection within 14 days.

3.2  A vibration alarm does not by itself require removal from service. The
     pump may remain in service where bearing temperature is within limit and
     no leakage is observed.

3.3  Loose baseplate anchor bolts shall be re-torqued to 95 Nm and the torque
     value recorded before the pump is returned to unattended operation.

4. INSTRUMENTATION

4.1  An illegible or damaged gauge shall be reported as a finding and replaced
     before the next routine inspection.

--- PAGE 3 ---

5. APPROVAL NOTE

5.1  An approval note raised from an inspection report shall state, for each
     finding, the SOP clause applied and the required action.

5.2  An approval note shall list every value the report recorded as
     "not recorded" as an open item. It shall not close a finding on a value
     that was never measured.

5.3  An approval note is countersigned before the pump is returned to
     unattended operation.
